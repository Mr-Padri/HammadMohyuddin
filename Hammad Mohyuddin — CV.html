About

AI is changing what a working day looks like, and I want to understand that change from the inside: how the technology works, what it can and can’t do, and where it fits into real workflows. My aim is to turn that understanding into deployments that enterprises adopt and teams rely on, and to sell them with honesty about what they deliver.

I pair a broad technical foundation (Python, JavaScript, React, databases, networking and cybersecurity) with commercial grounding in CRM, marketing and financial accounting, and several years of industry experience in medical billing, a field full of repetitive, rule-heavy work.
